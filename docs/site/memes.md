# Memecoins

Discover nad.fun launches, follow their bonding-curve progress and trade through the available path on Monad.

## Find new tokens

Open **Home → Markets → Memes** or the **Launch** section. Token cards show the indexed image, contract identity, available price or market-cap reference, and launch progress.

Market cap describes an implied token valuation. It is not the amount you can withdraw from liquidity or the proceeds of selling your balance.

## Before DEX migration

A token on a supported bonding curve uses that curve's buy or sell route. The order panel checks the token's current lifecycle and prepares a quote for your amount.

The curve's quoted amount can move as other people trade. Use the current quote and wallet request, rather than treating the card price as your fill price.

## After migration

When the indexed launch moves to a DEX, Rally checks the supported migrated route. Migration changes where execution happens; it does not guarantee that every order size has a route immediately.

A missing quote can mean insufficient liquidity, a route that is not available, or a provider that cannot currently confirm it. The exact contract address remains the token's identity across the transition.

## Charts and holders

Open the token panel to view its chart and indexed holders. Holder information appears only when a supported index can return it. An unavailable holder list is not evidence that the token has no holders.

## Launch your own community token

Use the Launch section to prepare an image, token settings and supported revenue policy. Account creation alone does not issue a token; launching is a separate wallet-approved action. See [Community tokens](community-tokens.md).
