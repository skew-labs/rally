# Fees and execution costs

The cost depends on the selected market, route and action. Use the current order or purchase panel for the amounts that apply.

## Network fees

Monad transactions use MON for gas. An allowance, trade, launch, claim or transfer can be a separate transaction with its own network fee. A reverted transaction may still consume gas.

## Spot and launch fees

DEX and bonding-curve routes use their own fee rules. Price impact and slippage affect output in addition to explicit fees. A larger order can receive a different effective price from the card reference.

Creating a community token can include initial liquidity or an initial buy, plus the applicable contract or venue fees. Check the launch's current payment summary before signing.

## Perpetual costs

Perpetual venues may charge order, funding, liquidation or withdrawal costs under their own rules. Collateral deposited into a venue is not itself a trading fee. Consult the selected venue's terms and current order details.

## Prediction pools

A Castora pool shows its fixed stake and pool fee. The stake participates under the pool's settlement rules. It is not a quoted purchase of a secondary Yes/No token.

## Algorithm subscriptions

A paid feed displays its USDC price, version, recipient and prepaid access term. Current terms are **30 days**, without automatic renewal.

A creator's buyback allocation divides that received revenue under the configured policy; it is a different revenue source from a venue's trading fees. See [Algorithms](algorithms.md) and [Community tokens](community-tokens.md).

## Missing or changing costs

If a current route or estimate is unavailable, refresh it before signing. This documentation does not substitute a universal fixed fee for the actual route.
