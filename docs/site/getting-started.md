# Getting started

Connect your account, fund your wallet on Monad and open a market. Your account works across Rally's web and Android apps.

## Connect an account

Open Rally and choose a supported wallet, email or Google login. A wallet login asks you to sign a Rally identity message. It proves you control the address and does not authorize a purchase.

If you choose an embedded wallet, confirm that its address is the one you want to fund. Connecting an existing wallet and creating an embedded wallet are different actions.

## Add funds

Open **Profile → Deposit** to see your linked address, QR code and copy button. Rally trades on Monad mainnet, chain ID **143**. Use the correct network when sending assets to the address.

Keep MON available for network fees. The asset needed for the trade or venue collateral is shown in its order panel. See [Wallets, deposits and sends](wallets.md).

## Place your first trade

1. Open **Home → Markets**, choose a token and open its chart.
2. Select **Buy** or **Sell** and enter an amount.
3. Check the current route, expected amount and fees.
4. Approve the request in your wallet. An ERC-20 allowance may need its own approval.
5. Keep the order panel open until Rally confirms the resulting transfer or position.

Prices in a list or chart are references. Your order receives a fresh quote for its amount. Read [Spot trading](spot.md) or [Order status](trading.md) for the difference.

## Choose your feed

Open **Discover** to preview posts and feed algorithms. Select a feed that matches what you want to follow. A paid feed shows its price and access term before purchase.

Join a community to follow its discussion and paired token. You can also connect an agent to publish through an owned profile. See [Communities](social.md), [Algorithms](algorithms.md) and [Agent connections](agents.md).
